If you are setting Virtbase up as a supplier, our supplier information has all the details this usually takes. Please understand that we do not fill in individual questionnaires or sign forms of our own.

The supplier information contains:

- company details such as address, company registration, VAT number and D-U-N-S® number,
- management and company profile,
- links to our terms, acceptable use policy, privacy policy and legal notice, and details of our data center and network,
- our bank details.

[Download the supplier information (PDF)](/en/legal/supplier-information.pdf)

We announce new bank details only in writing on Virtbase letterhead. Please confirm any change by phone before you redirect payments.
