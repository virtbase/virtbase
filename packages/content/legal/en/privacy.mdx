## 3. Visiting the website

### 3.1 Server log files

Our website and the customer portal are hosted by Vercel Inc. (see section 8). Every request processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, the address requested, the amount of data transferred, the status code, the browser identifier (user agent) and the previously visited page (referrer).

We need this data to deliver the website, fix errors and fend off attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Depending on their type, the log data is deleted automatically after one day and no later than 30 days.

### 3.2 Cookies and similar technologies

We only use cookies and local storage that are technically required to operate the website. These are:

| Name | Purpose | Storage period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session cookie of the customer portal | Keeps you signed in after logging in | 3 days |
| Session cache | Speeds up checking your login | 5 minutes |
| Last login method | Shows the login method you used last at your next login | 30 days |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Remembers the language you chose | 1 year |
| `__stripe_mid`, `__stripe_sid` (only during checkout) | Fraud prevention by Stripe | 1 year and 30 minutes respectively |

The legal basis for storing and reading them is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), and for the subsequent processing Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies and therefore have no cookie banner.

During checkout, our payment service provider Stripe embeds its own scripts, which may collect device and browser information for fraud prevention. See section 5.2 for details.

### 3.3 Error reports

If technical errors occur on the website or in the customer portal, information about the error (e.g. the affected page, browser, time, technical sequence of events) is sent to our own error tracking system (Sentry), which we operate on our own infrastructure in the SkyLink Data Center B.V. data centre in Eygelshoven (the Netherlands). Your IP address and other default personal data are not sent with it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an error-free platform (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Error reports are deleted after 90 days.

## 4. Customer account

### 4.1 Registration and login

To register, we need your name, your email address and a password. We only store your password as a cryptographic hash. You confirm your email address through a link we send you.

Each time you log in, we store a session with IP address, browser identifier and time. This lets you see in your account where you are logged in and end sessions. Sessions expire after 3 days.

You can optionally set up two-factor authentication, passkeys, SSH keys and API keys.

The legal basis is the performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Storing the session data is additionally based on our legitimate interest in the security of your account (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 4.2 Login with Discord, GitHub or Google

Instead of a password, you can also log in with an existing Discord, GitHub or Google account. You are redirected to the respective provider for this. After you agree there, we receive your name, your email address, an identifier of your account and, where applicable, your profile picture.

With Discord, we additionally request permission to show a connection (Linked Role) in your Discord profile, for example that you are a Virtbase customer. This information is updated regularly.

Providers:

- Discord Netherlands BV, Schiphol Boulevard 195, 1118 BG Schiphol, the Netherlands (for users in the EEA), or Discord Inc., 444 De Haro Street, Suite 200, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA
- GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA
- Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland

The providers also process data in the USA. Discord, GitHub and Google are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is your choice of this login method and thus the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can remove the connection at any time in your account or with the provider.

### 4.3 Account deletion and inactivity

You can delete your account at any time in the customer portal. After you confirm by email, the deletion is scheduled for 14 days. You can cancel it during this time. After that, we delete your data. Data we have to keep for tax and commercial law reasons (invoices, orders, payments) is anonymised or restricted until the retention periods expire.

For accounts without an active server that have not been used for 6 months, we announce the deletion by email. If you do not log in within 30 days, the account is deleted as described above. We remind you once more 7 days before the period ends. The legal basis is the principle of storage limitation (Art. 5(1)(e) GDPR).

## 6. Using the servers

### 6.1 Operation and management

To provide your servers, we process the server configuration, assigned IP addresses, hostnames you choose (reverse DNS), firewall rules, backups, uploaded ISO images, the operating system detected in the server and status data (e.g. utilisation). The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

The servers are located in our data centre at SkyLink Data Center B.V. in Eygelshoven (the Netherlands). Incoming traffic is filtered for protection against DDoS attacks by aurologic GmbH, Robert-Bosch-Straße 25, 63225 Langen, Germany.

### 6.2 Network and traffic data

For billing, to secure network operation and to detect attacks and abuse, we record traffic data (sFlow samples and traffic statistics with IP addresses, ports and data volumes). This data is deleted after 30 days at the latest. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure network operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and § 12 TDDDG.

We keep a record of which IP address was assigned to which customer and when, even after the contract ends, so that we can correctly attribute abuse reports and requests from authorities about past periods. After the contract ends, we only keep which address was assigned when; your notes on it are deleted. We also delete this record 12 months after the assignment ends.

### 6.3 AI firewall assistant

In the customer portal, an AI assistant can suggest firewall rules to you. Only when you actively use this feature do we send your server's existing firewall rules (including any IP addresses in them), the open ports and services, the detected operating system and your input to:

- Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA (routing through the AI Gateway)
- Anthropic, PBC, 548 Market Street, PMB 90375, San Francisco, CA 94104, USA (the Claude AI model)

The data is processed in the USA. For Vercel, the basis is its certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; for Anthropic, standard contractual clauses. Vercel does not store the requests after processing them. Anthropic does not store the data (zero data retention) and does not use it to train its models. The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), as you trigger the feature yourself.

### 6.4 Discord bot

You can manage your servers through our Discord bot. To do so, you link your Discord account to your customer account. We only store the link between your Discord identifier and your customer account. Commands and replies are transmitted through Discord and are subject to the privacy policy of Discord Inc. (USA). The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### 6.5 iOS app

You manage your servers with our app for iPhone and iPad. The app communicates with our interface (app.virtbase.com) exclusively over encrypted connections. The information in this privacy policy on the customer account and on using the servers applies accordingly.

Your access key is stored only on your device, in the protected iOS Keychain; it can only be read while the device is unlocked and is not included in device backups. The app's widget uses the same key. When you sign out of the app, the key is deleted.

The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising tools, no crash reporting system of its own, and sends no push notifications.

When you download the app from the App Store or through TestFlight, Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland, processes data under its own responsibility. If you have agreed in the iOS settings to share analytics data with app developers, Apple provides us with anonymised crash and usage statistics.

## 9. Handling abuse and requests from authorities

As a hosting provider, we are legally required to handle notices of illegal content (Art. 16 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Digital Services Act), to carry out removal orders for terrorist content (Regulation (EU) 2021/784) and to comply with justified requests for information from authorities.

In doing so, we process:

- the details in the report (e.g. name and email address of the person reporting, description, evidence),
- the affected IP addresses, times and servers,
- the data of the customer to whom the IP address was assigned at the time in question,
- our communication with reporters and customers, and the measures taken.

Reports reach us by email, through automated systems and through services such as AbuseIPDB (AbuseIPDB LLC, 562 Independence Road, East Stroudsburg, PA 18301, USA). We query AbuseIPDB as to whether there are reports about our IP addresses. We do not report any IP addresses to AbuseIPDB ourselves.

To pre-sort incoming reports, we use an AI model (Claude from Anthropic, PBC, through the Vercel AI Gateway, USA). The report is sent in shortened form, is not stored by Anthropic (zero data retention) and is not used for training; the transfer is based on standard contractual clauses. The model suggests a classification but makes no decisions. Every measure against a customer is triggered either by a rule we have defined or by a person. No automated decision within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

The legal bases are compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in protecting our infrastructure and third parties against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Repeated violations by the same customer are considered together for 90 days. If a customer deletes their account, the data on their abuse cases is deleted with it. Otherwise, we keep data on an abuse case until 12 months after the case is closed.
