Your name, email address and password are on the <Ui>General</Ui> page of your account settings. To open it, select your avatar in the customer portal and then <Ui>Account</Ui>, or go straight to [Account > General](https://app.virtbase.com/account/settings).

## Change your display name

Under <Ui>Display Name</Ui>, enter the new name (up to 32 characters) and select <Ui>Save</Ui>. We use it to greet you in our emails. The name on your invoices is the one in the billing address you enter when paying.

## Change your email address

1. Under <Ui>Change Email</Ui>, enter the new address and select <Ui>Save</Ui>.
2. Open the confirmation email we send to the **new** address and confirm.

Until you confirm, your old address stays active. Once the change is made, we notify your old address too, so you would notice if someone else changed it.

## Change your password

1. Under <Ui>Change Password</Ui>, enter your <Ui>Current Password</Ui> and the <Ui>New Password</Ui>.
2. Select <Ui>Save</Ui>.

A password needs 8 to 64 characters, with at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter and one digit.

This changes the password for the customer portal. To change the root password of a server, see [How do I reset my server's root password?](/en/help/article/how-to-reset-root-password)

## Forgot your password?

1. On the [sign-in page](https://app.virtbase.com/login), select <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui>.
2. Enter your email address and select <Ui>Send reset link</Ui>.
3. Open the email within 10 minutes and choose a new password.

Resetting your password signs you out on every device. If you prefer, you can also sign in without a password: select <Ui>Log in with email</Ui> on the sign-in page to receive a one-time sign-in link, valid for 5 minutes.

## Change the language

Select <Ui>Language</Ui> in the sidebar of the customer portal and choose English, Deutsch, Français or Nederlands. Our emails to you use the same language.

## Find your user ID

<Ui>Your User ID</Ui> is shown at the bottom of the <Ui>General</Ui> page. Support may ask for it to identify your account; select the copy button next to it.

## If it didn't work

- **The confirmation email does not arrive:** check your spam folder and that the new address is spelled correctly, then try again.
- **The current password is not accepted:** use <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui> on the sign-in page to set a new one.
