## Where do I find my invoices?

Every payment creates an invoice. You receive it as a PDF by email, and you find all of them under [Invoices](https://app.virtbase.com/invoices) in the customer portal, with the invoice number, amount, date and payment date. Select <Ui>Download</Ui> to get the PDF.

The <Ui>Dashboard</Ui> also shows your <Ui>Latest Invoices</Ui>.

## Which payment methods can I use?

- <Ui>Card</Ui>, <Ui>SEPA Direct Debit</Ui> and <Ui>PayPal</Ui>, processed securely by Stripe.
- **Cryptocurrency**, for orders of €5.00 or more, through <Ui>Pay anonymously with cryptocurrency</Ui>.

For [automatic renewal](/en/help/article/how-to-set-up-automatic-renewal), you need a saved card. Cryptocurrency cannot be used for automatic renewals; you can still renew by hand with it.

Saved payment methods are under [Account > Billing](https://app.virtbase.com/account/settings/billing). We only ever see the card brand and the last four digits.

## Are prices shown with VAT?

Yes. All prices include statutory VAT where it applies. The VAT is calculated from the billing address you enter when paying, and shown on the invoice. All prices are in euros.

## How do discounts work?

Running promotions are applied automatically at checkout; there are no coupon codes to enter. The price you pay for renewals is fixed when you order, so a later price change on the website does not change what you pay for an existing server. If the price of your plan ever changes, we tell you before the next automatic payment.

## When is a payment for SEPA Direct Debit or cryptocurrency complete?

These payments take longer to arrive. Until they do, the order shows <Ui>Payment is being processed</Ui>, and the server is set up or renewed once the payment has arrived. A <Term id="sepa-mandate">SEPA mandate</Term> can take a day or two to be confirmed.

## Can I get a refund?

When you order, you agree that the server is provided immediately. Your statutory right of withdrawal therefore ends once the server has been set up, as described in the [Revocation Policy](/en/legal/revocation).

We do not refund servers that work as intended. If something went wrong on our side, or there is another reason, [contact support](/en/contact) first and describe what happened. We decide each case individually.

## How do I stop paying for a server?

[Cancel its subscription](/en/help/article/how-to-set-up-automatic-renewal#cancel-the-subscription), or simply do not renew it. The server runs until the end of the term you have paid for. Nothing is charged after that.

## My invoice has the wrong address. Can it be corrected?

The invoice uses the billing address you entered when paying. If it is wrong, [contact support](/en/contact) with the invoice number and the correct address.
