---
title: "Two-factor authentication (2FA)"
description: "Signing in with a second proof besides your password, usually a code from an app on your phone."
---

With two-factor authentication on, a stolen password is not enough to get into your account. Virtbase supports authenticator apps such as 1Password, Google Authenticator and Microsoft Authenticator.

See [How do I secure my account with two-factor authentication and passkeys?](/en/help/article/how-to-set-up-two-factor-authentication)
