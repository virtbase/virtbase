---
title: "root"
description: "The administrator account on Linux, allowed to do everything on the system."
---

You log in to a new Linux server as `root`. Its password is the one you chose when ordering, or the one sent to you by email; Virtbase does not store it. You can [reset it](/en/help/article/how-to-reset-root-password) in the customer portal.

On Windows, the equivalent account is called `Administrator`.
