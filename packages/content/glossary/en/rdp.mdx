---
title: "RDP"
description: "Remote Desktop Protocol: how you connect to the desktop of a Windows server."
---

RDP shows a Windows server's desktop on your computer, the way [SSH](#ssh) gives you a command line on Linux. It uses [port](#port) 3389, which must be open in the [firewall](#firewall). Remote Desktop clients are built into Windows and available for macOS, Linux and phones.
