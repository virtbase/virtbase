---
title: "QEMU guest agent"
description: "A small service inside your server that lets the customer portal read and change things in the operating system."
---

The guest agent (`qemu-guest-agent`) is what allows Virtbase to reset passwords, show disk usage, detect the operating system and a firewall inside the server, and take consistent [snapshots](#snapshot). It is installed on every operating system you can order.

See [What do my server's status, power buttons and graphs mean?](/en/help/article/about-server-status#the-qemu-guest-agent)
