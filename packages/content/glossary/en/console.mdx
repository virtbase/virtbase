---
title: "Console"
description: "Your server's screen and keyboard in the browser, reachable even when its network is not."
---

The console connects to the virtual machine directly rather than over its network, so it works when [SSH](#ssh) does not: after a firewall mistake, with a broken network configuration, or while a system boots or installs.

You open it on the <Ui>Console</Ui> tab of a server. See [How do I use the browser console?](/en/help/article/how-to-use-console)
