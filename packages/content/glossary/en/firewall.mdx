---
title: "Firewall"
description: "A filter that decides which network traffic may reach a server and leave it."
---

Every Virtbase server has a firewall in front of it that you configure in the customer portal. A firewall inside the server, such as ufw or firewalld, can apply as well; traffic then has to pass both.

Rules allow or block traffic by direction, [port](#port), protocol and source address. See [How do I use the firewall on my server?](/en/help/article/how-to-use-server-firewall)
