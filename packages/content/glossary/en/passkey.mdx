---
title: "Passkey"
description: "A way to sign in with your fingerprint, face, device PIN or security key instead of a password."
---

A passkey is stored on your device and only works on the website it was created for, so it cannot be guessed or phished. You can add several passkeys to your Virtbase account.

See [How do I secure my account with two-factor authentication and passkeys?](/en/help/article/how-to-set-up-two-factor-authentication)
