---
title: "DDoS attack"
description: "An attempt to make a server unreachable by flooding it with traffic from many machines at once."
---

A distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack sends so much traffic that a server or its network can no longer answer real visitors. Every Virtbase server is protected by always-on DDoS filtering at no extra cost.

See [What DDoS protection does Virtbase use?](/en/help/article/about-ddos-protection)
